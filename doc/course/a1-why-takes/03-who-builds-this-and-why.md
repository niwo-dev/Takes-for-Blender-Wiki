## What to show, in order

1. Cold open: the demo, working, no talking. The proof comes before the claims.
2. Real product work. One project, with a number.
3. What was tried before building anything.
4. What was missing: a hierarchy rather than a flat list of saved states.
5. Who is building it.
6. The AI beat. No code on screen — it is a trust claim, not a technical one.
7. Built from the problem inward, not the data model outward.
8. Ships often, and what that does and does not mean.
9. The ask. Problems first, support second.

## Set up before you record

- The full demo, working, for the cold open.
- Stills of real product visualisation work.
- The roadmap board on screen for the 'ships often' beat.

## Where to be careful

- Say the AI part once, calmly, and move on. The longer it is defended the more it sounds like it needs defending.
- No crash claim. 'Quick and steady on files with a lot going on' cannot be falsified by one bad afternoon.
- Every number must be real. A placeholder read aloud is worse than no number.
