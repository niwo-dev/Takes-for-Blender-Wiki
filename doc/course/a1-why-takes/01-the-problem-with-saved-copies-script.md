## 1:02 — Name the real cost

**Screen.** Back to the folder. Hold on the full list.

> That is the real cost, and it is not the disk space.

**[HOLD 1s]**

## Before you record

From the context sheet, repeated here so it is in one place:

- A folder of decoy files with believable names and a spread of dates.
- One finished demo file with at least three rounds that look clearly different.
- Sidebar open on the Takes tab. Nothing else on screen.
- Blender at a clean startup size, so the recording crops cleanly to 16:9.
