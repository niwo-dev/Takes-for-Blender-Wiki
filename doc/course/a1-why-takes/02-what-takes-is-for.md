**Description**  
What this add-on is for, and what it is not for.

## What this video has to do

- Draw the line around what the add-on manages: the stage, not the story.
- Say plainly what it does not do, so nobody arrives expecting an editor.
- Give the one sentence a viewer would repeat to a colleague.

## What the viewer already knows

- The problem from A1.1. Nothing else.

## What they can do afterwards

- They can tell whether this add-on is for the work they do.

## What to show, in order

1. One product, one shot. Change the camera. Change the world. Change the finish. Same file.
2. Then the counter-example: a timeline of many different shots — say that is a different job and a different tool.
3. Close on the five things it manages: camera, world, materials, action, render settings.

## Set up before you record

- The demo file, with at least two cameras, two worlds and two finishes.
- A second file or a still showing a multi-shot sequence for the contrast.

## Where to be careful

- Name no competitor as worse. The wiki's Comparing page treats them as neighbours and partners; the video must match it.
- Resist listing features. Five words is the whole list.

## Out of scope

Name it and move on. Do not explain it here.

- Who builds it — next video.
- Every feature. This is the shape, not the tour.
