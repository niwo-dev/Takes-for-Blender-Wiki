# Panels

Everything lives in the **3D Viewport sidebar**: press ++n++, then click the **Takes** tab. The Navigation Panel sits on top and picks which panel shows below it.

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**Navigation Panel**](navigation_panel.md) | The always-visible header — shared toggles, warnings, and the panel switcher. |
| [**Inspector Panel**](inspector_panel.md) | A watchlist of your managed and pinned objects, with their animation. |
| [**Context Properties**](context_properties.md) | The Takes Tree and each layer's overrides — cameras, worlds, actions, presets. |
| [**Variant Tree**](variant_tree.md) | Where you build product variants and pick who wears which look. |
| [**Globals Panel**](../features/globals.md) | Project-wide settings, presets, rules, tags and variants in one place. |
| [**Keyboard Shortcuts**](hotkeys.md) | Every shortcut on one page. |

??? info "How the panel switcher works"
    The Navigation Panel's bottom row is a row of buttons. Clicking one shows
    that panel and hides the others.

    A **link toggle** sits between Context and Inspector. Switch it on and the
    two open together. Switch it off and you are back to one panel at a time.

!!! tip "Looking for a preference, not a panel?"
    The add-on's options live in Blender's Preferences — start at the
    [Preferences overview](../preferences/index.md).
