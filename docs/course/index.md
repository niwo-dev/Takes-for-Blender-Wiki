# The Course

80 short videos, in order, built on the pages you already have. One problem per video, none longer than eight minutes.

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A0.1">Learn Takes for Blender: the whole course in four minutes</div>

What you will be able to do, the four stages, the bottle everything is built on, how to watch, and how your ideas steer where it goes next.

## :material-rocket-launch: Takes is growing, on purpose

Takes ships often, so buttons get clearer names and features grow. A video may show a button that has since moved.

The wiki pages under every lesson are kept current. Check there if something looks different.

That is not the same as rough. Takes is quick and steady, and it carries real product work without falling over.

**Your ideas steer it.** The problem you hit is the one worth fixing — see [A8 · Getting help](a8-getting-help.md).

## :material-stairs: The four stages

6 h 18 in all. Stage A alone is enough to run a real job.

| Stage | What you get | Videos | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Think in Takes** | Everything you need to work with Takes for real. | 36 | 2 h 48 |
| **B · Build looks and shots** | Cameras, lighting, finishes, presets and tags. | 20 | 1 h 36 |
| **C · Ship it** | Names that write themselves, batch render, a whole product. | 16 | 1 h 17 |
| **D · Power tools** | Hotkeys and pies, the sequencer, the assistant. | 7 | 33 min |
