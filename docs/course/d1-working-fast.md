# D1 · Working fast

4 lessons · 18 min · the idea here is that **your hands stay where they are**.

## 1. Hotkeys and click modifiers<span class="tks-min">5 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="D1.1">Takes hotkeys and the tree click modifiers</div>

Add, rename, delete and group from the keyboard. Ctrl, Shift and Alt on a chevron reach children, siblings or the whole tree.

Read more: [Keyboard Shortcuts](../interface/hotkeys.md)

## 2. The six pies<span class="tks-min">5 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="D1.2">Pie menus in Takes: navigation, render, modes, keyframes</div>

Flick a direction instead of hunting for a button. Only the Navigation pie is on out of the box; the other five are yours to switch on.

Read more: [Pie Menus](../features/pie_menus.md)

## 3. Bookmarking a buried property<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="D1.3">Bookmark any property and edit it from one shelf</div>

Right-click the value you keep hunting for and bookmark it. Every one you pick lands on a single shelf in the Inspector.

Read more: [Bookmark a Property](../workflows/bookmark_a_property.md)

## 4. Changing twelve rows at once<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="D1.4">Change twelve layers at once: Multiselect and Push to Selected</div>

Turn on Multiselect, tick the rows, and act on all of them. Push to Selected fans one value out across the lot.

Read more: [Tag Library](../features/tags.md#push-tag-to-selected)
