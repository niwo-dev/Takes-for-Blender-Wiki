2 lessons · 9 min · **The video editor and your tools follow the shot**.

## 1. Video editor shows the wrong shot? The fix <span class="tks-min">5 <span class="tks-min__u">min</span></span> { #1-video-editor-shows-the-wrong-shot-the-fix }
<div class="tks-video" data-video="D2.1">Video Sequencer shows the wrong take? Playhead follow explained</div>

A scene strip only shows whichever take is live. With the video editor open, Takes applies the take of the strip under the playhead, so the preview matches the render.

Read more: [Video Sequencer](../features/sequencer.md)

## 2. Stop snap and pivot resetting when you switch <span class="tks-min">4 <span class="tks-min__u">min</span></span> { #2-stop-snap-and-pivot-resetting-when-you-switch }
<div class="tks-video" data-video="D2.2">Snap settings reset when you switch scenes? Viewport Sync</div>

Blender keeps snap, pivot and similar tool settings per scene. Viewport Sync copies a change to every scene, so switching never swaps your tools.

Read more: [Viewport Sync](../features/viewport_sync.md)
