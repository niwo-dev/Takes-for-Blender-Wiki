# A4 · The Cascade

6 lessons · 25 min · the idea here is that **a deeper level always wins**.

## 1. Deeper wins<span class="tks-min">5 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.1">The Cascade explained: set it once, every take inherits it</div>

Set a camera at Scene and every shot uses it. Give one shot its own and that shot wins. The rest still follow.

Read more: [The Cascade](../features/cascade.md)

## 2. The four clicks<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.2">Bright or dim icon? The four clicks that set, open and clear an override</div>

Click picks a value. Shift opens the full popover. Alt clears this level. Shift and Alt together clear everything below too.

Read more: [The Cascade](../features/cascade.md)

## 3. Making data per level<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.3">How to create a camera, world, action or compositor per tier in one click</div>

The **+** in a popover makes the datablock, names it from your template, protects it and assigns it here.

Read more: [The Cascade](../features/cascade.md)

## 4. Every override on one page<span class="tks-min">3 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.4">Context Properties: every override of one layer on one page</div>

The Context panel lists what the active layer resolved, and which level each value came from.

Read more: [Context Properties](../interface/context_properties.md)

## 5. Blender's own pickers still work<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.5">Why did my camera change? Blender's picker vs the Takes picker</div>

Pick a camera the Blender way and Takes adopts it onto your adopt level, so a switch cannot lose it. You choose which level that is.

Read more: [Workflow Preferences](../preferences/workflow.md)

## 6. Planning your tree<span class="tks-min">5 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A4.6">How to plan your tree - what belongs at Global, Scene and View Layer</div>

Put a value at the widest level where it is still true. Override below only where it stops being true.

Read more: [The Cascade](../features/cascade.md) · [Which Tool for the Job](../features/which_tool.md)
