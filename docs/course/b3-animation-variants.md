# B3 · Animation variants

2 lessons · 9 min · the idea here is that **one motion per layer, and Rest State between them**.

## 1. One animation per View Layer<span class="tks-min">6 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="B3.1">Spin, tilt, explode: one animation per View Layer</div>

Make a layer per motion and group them. Animate in each. Switching away sends the objects home to their rest pose.

Read more: [Make Animation Variants](../workflows/animation_variants.md) · [Rest State](../features/rest_state.md)

## 2. Rendering the whole group<span class="tks-min">3 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="B3.2">How to render a group of animations at once</div>

Turn on Multiselect, tick the layers you want, and render. Smart Output names each file after its layer.

Read more: [Batch Render Your Takes](../workflows/batch_rendering.md)
