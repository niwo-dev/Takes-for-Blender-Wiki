# A3 · Your first take

4 lessons · 19 min · the idea here is that **you branch instead of duplicating**.

## 1. Two takes, two angles<span class="tks-min">6 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A3.1">How to make your first take - two camera angles, one click to switch</div>

Build a take, give it a camera, add a second, frame a new angle. Click between them and the viewport follows.

Read more: [Your First Take](../getting_started/first_take.md)

## 2. The review loop<span class="tks-min">5 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A3.2">How to run a review loop in Blender: notes and New Take from Here</div>

Write what the review asked for in the take's note. **New Take from Here** copies everything and starts the next round with an empty note.

Read more: [Takes - the Review Loop](../features/vl_versions.md) · [Switch Between Takes](../workflows/switch_takes.md)

## 3. Take or View Layer?<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A3.3">Take or new View Layer? How to decide</div>

Same shot, another go: a Take. Different objects, passes or motion: a View Layer.

Read more: [Which Tool for the Job](../features/which_tool.md)

## 4. Renaming and tidying<span class="tks-min">4 min</span>

<div class="tks-video" data-video="A3.4">How to rename, reorder, duplicate and delete takes safely</div>

Rename inline, move rows with the arrows, and switch a take off to fall back to the layer. Deleting the last take gives the layer back its own settings.

Read more: [Context Properties](../interface/context_properties.md)
